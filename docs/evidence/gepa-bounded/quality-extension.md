# Post-selection quality extension

Added on the owner's explicit request to emphasize GEPA benefits beyond cost,
after selection was frozen and while final comparison calls were running.
This is a supplementary, deliberately seeded correction diagnostic, not a new
optimization objective, blind bug-discovery score or independent population test.
The original protocol, candidates and primary comparison remain unchanged.

For each held-out component, take repetition zero's immutable baseline and make
a separate copy with three predeclared wrong verdicts/reasons. Do not change the
source, oracle, claim inventory or original report. Send the identical damaged
report to fixed and frozen GEPA review. Timer order fixed/GEPA; Once order GEPA/fixed.
No-review is the uncorrected diagnostic input, not a claim the baseline model
actually made these errors. All original baseline outputs remain immutable.

Timer: claim 02 incorrectly says Stop waits for a running callback; claim 03
incorrectly denies rescheduling after start; claim 07 improperly decides application
retry policy. Once: claim 01 incorrectly treats copies of the returned function
as independent once state; claim 03 incorrectly retries a panicking original;
claim 07 improperly decides application retry policy. All other verdicts stay
correct. Three errors and four correct controls per component.

Measure seeded errors corrected, correct controls retained, errors introduced,
policy questions reopened, complete attributed corrections and total extra Codex
cost including structural repair. Four review calls plus at most one structural
repair per call. They remain inside the experiment's original 40-launch/$10
controller ceilings. No additional provider drafting or paid re-optimization.
Do not mix these artificial-input scores into the primary 42 observations per arm.
Both prompts may repair all errors; that proves correction capability on these
cases, not a GEPA-specific improvement over the fixed review.
