## Remaining limits

The overwrite probes are sequential. They leave concurrent wrapper mutation and general support for replacement unresolved. The direct receiver test copies an unused source value. Go’s `sync.Mutex` documentation prohibits copying a mutex after first use. The test gives no guarantee for broader value copying.

The lock-based reentry finding is untested. Close deadline schedules around a callback that outlasts expiry and races between unwind and retry are source inferences. No deliberately blocked callback was left running. Panic probes use non-nil panic values. Closed-window probes bracket elapsed expiry with a margin, leaving exact timestamp equality to the source’s strict `Before` comparison. The caller-departure probe observes the pending interval, then releases each worker to inspect its buffered result.
