# Open questions

The README leaves lifecycle policy undecided. These questions describe the contract gaps raised by serialized observations. `explicit-requirement` — README.md:5–8.

- **Q1 · A after rebinding.** Should a completion credit a different account whose epoch matches the admission epoch? How should the original request and replacement success be attributed? When the replacement starts with a different epoch and later changes to match, should the same answer apply? See F07, F13, F45, F53, F67, and F73.
- **Q2 · Epoch change and restoration.** Should A and C continue to accept an outstanding completion once a numeric epoch is restored? What should happen during the mismatch, and does an earlier successful or skipped call matter? See F19, F21, F43, F44, F73, and F74.
- **Q3 · C after identity changes.** Should a different account be ineligible even when its epoch matches? After detachment or rebinding, should restoring the original pointer make the callback eligible once the saved epoch also matches? See F09, F15, F33, F46, F55, F69, and F74.
- **Q4 · B, E, and F after lifecycle changes.** Should these callbacks continue to credit the admitted account after target replacement, detachment, or an epoch change on that account? Is their common accounting effect intended given E's distinct receiver form? See F08, F11, F12, F20, F23, F24, F32, F34, F35, F65, F68, F71, and F72.
- **Q5 · D's key and map lifecycle.** Should completion credit a different account installed under the same key, including after deletion, a nil value, or wholesale map replacement? Should the epoch of the current occupant affect eligibility? What result belongs to a callback invoked with the entry unavailable, and should that call affect a later invocation once the key returns? See F10, F16, F22, F36–F42, F56, and F70.
- **Q6 · Invocation count and retirement.** Should a completion become ineligible after its first credited call, a skipped call, or a panic? Who is responsible for holding, discarding, or explicitly invoking the callback again? See F31–F41, F43–F52, F73, and F74.
- **Q7 · Caller departure.** Should the recorded request remain when the initiating side stops waiting? If a holder invokes the callback later, should the result still follow the operation's observed attribution and eligibility rules? See F25–F30, F36, F38, F40, F47–F52, and F67–F72.
- **Q8 · Invalid inputs and detach.** What is the contract for nil Port targets and missing or nil Registry entries at admission, versus unavailability after valid admission? Should the contract cover nil receivers or nil Port arguments as well? See F31–F41 and F59–F64.
- **Q9 · Overlap and resets.** When the newer admission completes first, should the older completion be able to add a second success on the newly bound account? If a captured account's counters were reset between admission and completion, which accounting period should ultimately include the completion? See F53–F58 and F66.
