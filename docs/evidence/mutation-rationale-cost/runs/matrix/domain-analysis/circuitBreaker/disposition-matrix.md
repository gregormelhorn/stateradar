# Disposition matrix

Abstraction: Constructed breaker modes. StateHalfOpenSaturated means admitted count at budget with no further admissions until an outcome causes transition. Stored Open may be due for lazy reconciliation. Outstanding work and synchronization are orthogonal. Admission and completion columns show the post-reconciliation decision; ReconcileTime carries the refresh transition.

- G-01 proven: admission mode and requests versus max, gobreaker.go:283-290. At equality reject in half-open; one below admits final slot.
- G-02 proven: generation equal applies and unequal discards, gobreaker.go:298-306. This proof begins after time reconciliation.
- G-03 proven: half-open successes after increment versus maximum, gobreaker.go:315-318. At maximum close. Both half-open cells model failure as their representative target; success-close edges are modeled and traced separately.
- G-04 proven: default trip threshold, gobreaker.go:193. Five stays and six trips.
- G-05 not-formalizable: clock: time.Time.Before samples runtime time; before deadline or equality stays and strictly later refreshes, gobreaker.go:337-342.
- G-06 not-formalizable: external-call: policy callback may panic or stall before returning either Boolean value, gobreaker.go:326-327.
- G-07 proven: constructor zero and sign defaults, gobreaker.go:147-175.
